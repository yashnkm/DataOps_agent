DBS BANK - VISA PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT
Contract Number: DBS-VISA-2020-001
Effective Date: September 13, 2020

ADDENDUM TO PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT

This Addendum ("Addendum") is entered into as of September 13, 2020, by and between DBS BANK ("Participant") and VISA ("Service Provider").

This Addendum modifies the Participation Agreement dated [Insert Original Agreement Date] ("Agreement") and is incorporated therein by reference. All capitalized terms not defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Agreement.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the parties wish to amend certain provisions of the Agreement to reflect updated terms and conditions;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. Scope of Services

The following services shall be added to the Scope of Services:

• Expanded Network Access: PULSE shall provide access to an expanded network of international ATMs and POS terminals.

• Enhanced Fraud Protection: Real-time fraud detection and alerting services shall be included as part of the standard service package.

2. Fees

Effective September 13, 2020, the following fee structure shall replace the existing fee schedule:

• Standard transaction processing fee shall be discounted by $0.005 per transaction
• The fixed fee component shall be discounted by $0.025 per transaction
• The US Issuer ATM fee shall be discounted by $0.15 per transaction
• Debit Protect service fees shall be waived entirely
• Network security fee remains at $200 per month
• Acquirer ATM fee shall be set at $0.005 per transaction

3. International Transaction Fees

For cross-border and international transactions:
• Cross-border processing fee: $1.50 per transaction (discounted from $2.00)
• Currency conversion fee: 1.2% of transaction amount
• International ATM access fee: $0.50 per transaction

4. Interchange Fees

Interchange fees for card-present transactions:
• Debit card transactions: 1.2% + $0.10 (discounted from 1.5% + $0.15)
• Credit card transactions: 2.2% + $0.20 (discounted from 2.5% + $0.25)
• Premium card transactions: 3.1% + $0.30

5. Reporting and Compliance

Participant shall submit quarterly compliance and performance reports to VISA, beginning [Insert Start Date].

The reports shall include:
• Transaction volume statistics
• Fee reconciliation reports
• Fraud detection metrics
• Network availability reports
• Customer satisfaction scores

6. Service Level Agreements

VISA commits to the following service levels:
• Network uptime: 99.9% monthly availability
• Transaction processing time: Maximum 3 seconds for authorization
• Dispute resolution: 30 days maximum resolution time
• Customer support: 24/7 multilingual support

7. Modification to Article 3 – Term and Termination

The term of the Agreement is hereby extended by 2 additional years, resulting in a new termination date of [Insert New Termination Date].

8. Miscellaneous

• Entire Agreement: This Addendum, together with the Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

• Conflict: In the event of any conflict between this Addendum and the Agreement, the terms of this Addendum shall prevail.

• Ratification: Except as expressly modified herein, all other terms and conditions of the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.
