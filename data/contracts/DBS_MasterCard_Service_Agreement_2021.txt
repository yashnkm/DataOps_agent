DBS BANK - MASTERCARD SERVICE AGREEMENT
Contract Number: DBS-MC-2021-001
Effective Date: January 15, 2021

MASTERCARD ACQUIRING AND PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into between DBS BANK ("Acquirer") and MASTERCARD INTERNATIONAL ("MasterCard") effective January 15, 2021.

TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. Service Scope

MasterCard agrees to provide the following services:
• Transaction processing and authorization
• Settlement and clearing services
• Fraud monitoring and prevention
• Chargeback and dispute management
• Regulatory compliance support

2. Fee Structure

The following fees apply to all transactions processed under this agreement:

Transaction Processing Fees:
• ATM withdrawal processing: $0.008 per transaction (discounted from $0.012)
• Point-of-sale processing: $0.140 per transaction (discounted from $0.180)
• Online/CNP transactions: $0.160 per transaction
• Contactless payments: $0.120 per transaction

Interchange Fees:
• Standard debit cards: 1.1% + $0.08 (discounted from 1.45% + $0.12)
• Standard credit cards: 2.1% + $0.18
• Premium credit cards: 3.2% + $0.25
• Corporate cards: 2.8% + $0.20

Monthly Service Fees:
• Account maintenance: $150 per month
• Fraud monitoring: $75 per month
• Compliance reporting: $50 per month
• Customer support: Included

3. Chargeback Fees

Chargeback processing fees:
• Pre-arbitration: $25 per case
• Arbitration filing: $100 per case
• Representment: $15 per case
• Administrative fee: $10 per chargeback

4. International Transactions

Cross-border transaction fees:
• International processing: $1.25 per transaction
• Currency conversion: 1.0% of transaction amount
• Cross-border assessment: 0.8% of transaction amount

5. Volume Incentives

Volume-based discounts apply:
• Tier 1 (1M-5M transactions/month): 5% discount on processing fees
• Tier 2 (5M-10M transactions/month): 8% discount on processing fees
• Tier 3 (10M+ transactions/month): 12% discount on processing fees

6. Performance Standards

MasterCard commits to:
• 99.95% network availability
• Average authorization response time: 2.5 seconds
• Settlement completion: T+1 business day
• 24/7 technical support

7. Compliance Requirements

DBS BANK must maintain:
• PCI DSS Level 1 compliance
• Anti-money laundering programs
• Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures
• Monthly transaction monitoring reports
• Quarterly compliance audits

8. Data Security

Both parties agree to:
• Encrypt all cardholder data in transit and at rest
• Implement tokenization for stored payment data
• Conduct regular security assessments
• Report any data breaches within 24 hours
• Maintain cyber insurance coverage

9. Term and Termination

This agreement shall remain in effect for 3 years from the effective date, with automatic renewal for additional 1-year terms unless terminated by either party with 90 days written notice.

10. Dispute Resolution

All disputes shall be resolved through:
• Initial negotiation period: 30 days
• Mediation if negotiation fails: 60 days
• Binding arbitration as final resort
